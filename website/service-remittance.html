Remittance

Receive international remittance directly into your wallet, instantly.

Download AppCharges & Limits
🌍

Overview

Family abroad can send money that lands directly in your MuktoPay wallet within minutes of being sent through our exchange house and bank partners — no need to visit a branch to collect cash.

Benefits

✓

Instant credit once sent by a partner exchange house

✓

Competitive exchange rates

✓

SMS and app notification the moment funds arrive

✓

Use received funds immediately for any service

How it works

1

Share your registered mobile number with the sender abroad

2

Sender initiates transfer through a partner exchange house or bank

3

Funds are credited to your wallet automatically

4

You receive an instant notification with the amount

Eligibility

•

Active MuktoPay wallet with completed KYC

•

Sender must use a partner exchange house or bank channel
